    Road to the platinum

    Five stages, played in this order. The story comes first, because one meditation in Chapter 1 is the run's only missable — everything after the finale can be tidied up in free-roam.

      Missable trophies

      One trophy in this guide is flagged as potentially missable. The rest can all be earned after the story, in free-roam — but with no mission replay, the safe habit is a separate save at the start of every chapter.

      The save-slot habit

      Keep one save slot for each chapter start, plus a rolling slot before any stranger finale. If a spot or a strand ever turns out to be missable, you rewind one chapter instead of the whole run.
