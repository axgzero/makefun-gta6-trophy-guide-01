    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five phases, in the order to play them. The story comes first because one meditation in Chapter 1 is the run’s only missable — everything after the finale can be cleaned up in free-roam.

      Missable trophies

      There is one trophy this guide flags as potentially missable. Everything else can be earned after the story, in free-roam — but with no mission replay, the safe habit is a separate save at the start of every chapter.

      Save-slot habit

      Keep one save slot per chapter start, plus a rolling slot before any stranger finale. If a spot or a strand ever turns out to be missable, you rewind a chapter instead of the whole run.
